💡 The idea in 30 seconds

Everything is built from three simple things.

🎨
ColorsEach filament you buy or want, like Jade White PLA Basic.
🧾
OrdersWhat you bought, when, what it cost, and tracking.
🧵
SpoolsEach physical roll. Orders create them for you.

The life of a spool

Ordered→ On the way→ Sealed→ Open · 60% left→ Used up

You only ever move a spool along this line. Ordering adds to your inventory, using up takes away, and the counts take care of themselves.

🚀 Getting started

One time only, the first time you open it.

  1. Import your spreadsheet. On the empty Stock tab, tap Import my spreadsheet. It brings in your 31 colors and all six Bambu orders, checked against the receipts. It takes a minute or two to finish saving.
  2. Mark the May order as arrived. Its arrival date was never recorded, so it shows as on the way. On the Orders tab, open the May 7, 2026 order and tap Save & mark arrived.
  3. Tell it which spools are open. Everything imports as sealed. For each roll in use, tap the color and choose Open. Do the same with Used up for any that are finished.
  4. Set how much is left on the open ones (see Checking how much is left).
💾 Add it to your Home Screen. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen and it opens like an app, right at the printer.

✅ Everyday tasks

Tap a task to see the steps.

📦I just opened a new spool
  1. On the Stock tab, tap the color.
  2. Next to a sealed spool, tap Open. Today's date is saved as the day you opened it.
⚖️Checking how much is left on a roll
  1. Tap the color. Each open spool has a slider.
  2. Slide it, or type the grams, to match your best estimate:
    • the markings on the spool, or
    • the remaining % shown in Bambu Handy for that AMS slot.
  3. Let go and it saves, with today's date.
🔴 Under 20% left, a spool turns red and counts as running low. If it's your last one, it goes on the Shopping list.
🏁I finished a spool
  1. Tap the color, then tap Used up on that spool. Sliding it to 0 g offers to do the same.
  2. Your on-hand count drops by one. The spool moves into the Used up history on the Costs tab, which shows how many days it lasted.
🛒I ordered filament
  1. On the Orders tab, tap ＋ New order. Or tap Order more from any color, or start from the Shopping list.
  2. Add each color with its quantity and the sale price per spool from the receipt. The last price you paid fills in for you.
  3. Reusable spools or other non-filament items go under Other items. They count in the order total but not in your filament cost per kg.
  4. Tax is worked out at 6%. If the receipt is a cent different, type its exact figure in Tax $. Add the order number if you like.
  5. Tap Add order. The new spools show as Ordered, and any Want/Need flag on those colors clears.
Color not in the list yet? Pick ＋ New color… at the bottom of the dropdown.
🚚Tracking a package / it arrived
  1. Open the order and paste the tracking number under Tracking. UPS, FedEx and USPS numbers become tap-to-track links.
  2. When it arrives, tap Save & mark arrived, or fill in the Arrived date for each package if they came separately.
  3. The spools change from On the way to Sealed, and your on-hand count goes up.
⭐Remembering a color I want
  1. Tap the color, or add it with ＋ Add a filament color at the bottom of the Stock tab.
  2. Choose Want for "someday" or Need for "buy soon".
📝Making a shopping list
  1. The Shopping tab collects everything worth buying: Need and Want colors, anything out of stock, and last spools that are running low or already open.
  2. Untick anything you're skipping, then tap Start an order with selected. The order opens pre-filled with last-paid prices.
🧮What did that print cost?
  1. On the Costs tab, enter the grams from Bambu Studio or Handy.
  2. Leave it on Average of all, or pick the color to use what you actually paid for it.
✏️Fixing a mistake

Order: tap it to edit anything, including quantities. Lowering a quantity removes a sealed spool; open or used ones are never deleted. Delete order is at the bottom.

Color: tap it, then ✎ Edit color details to change the name, code or swatch color. A color can only be deleted once it has no spools.

Spool status: just tap a different button (Sealed / Open / Used up) to undo.

☁️ Where your data lives

  • Saved on your device first, so it works with no signal. Changes upload when you're back online.
  • Synced through the same Google backend as the driving log, so your phone and computer see the same inventory.
  • Your original filament spreadsheet isn't touched. It stays as an archive; the tracker is now the place to update.
